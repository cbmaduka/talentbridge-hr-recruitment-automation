# Google Form Configuration

## Objective

The Google Form serves as the primary entry point for all job applications submitted to TalentBridge HR Solutions.

It was designed to collect standardized candidate information while ensuring every application contains the details required for efficient screening and processing.

---

# Form Details

**Form Name**

TalentBridge HR Job Application Form

**Purpose**

Collect candidate information for recruitment and automatically trigger downstream recruitment workflows.

---

# Information Collected

The application form captures the following information:

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Position Applied For
- Years of Experience
- Preferred Work Arrangement
- Available Start Date
- Resume Link
- Candidate Summary (Why would you be a great fit?)

---

# Positions Available

The form allows candidates to apply for the following positions:

- Project Manager
- Customer Success Specialist
- HR Officer
- Administrative Assistant
- Operations Coordinator
- Business Analyst

---

# Validation

Required fields were configured to ensure complete applications are submitted before processing.

This minimizes incomplete records and improves data quality.

---

# Automation Trigger

A successful form submission automatically triggers the recruitment workflow in Zapier.

The submitted data is then processed without manual intervention.

---

# Business Benefits

- Standardized application process
- Consistent candidate information
- Reduced administrative effort
- Improved data quality
- Seamless integration with Google Sheets and Zapier
