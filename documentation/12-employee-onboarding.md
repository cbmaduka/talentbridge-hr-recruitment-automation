# Employee Onboarding Automation

## Objective

The Employee Onboarding Automation marks the final stage of the recruitment process.

When a candidate is moved to the **Hired** stage, the automation supports onboarding activities and ensures a consistent transition from recruitment to employment.

---

# Trigger

**Application**

Trello

**Event**

Card Moved to List

**List**

Hired

---

# Automated Actions

The automation performs the following actions:

- Detects when a candidate is hired
- Initiates onboarding activities
- Updates the recruitment workflow
- Standardizes employee onboarding

---

# Business Benefits

- Consistent onboarding process
- Reduced administrative effort
- Improved HR workflow
- Better recruitment lifecycle management

---

# Outcome

The recruitment process concludes with a structured onboarding workflow, ensuring successful candidates transition smoothly into the organization.
