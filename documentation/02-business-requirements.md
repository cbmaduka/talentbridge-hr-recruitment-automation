# Business Requirements

## Background

TalentBridge HR Solutions manages recruitment for multiple organizations and receives applications for a variety of positions.

The existing recruitment process relied heavily on manual work, resulting in delays, duplicate effort, and inconsistent communication with candidates.

---

# Problems Identified

- Manual review of applications
- Manual creation of candidate records
- Delayed confirmation emails
- Poor visibility into recruitment stages
- Time-consuming applicant tracking
- Increased administrative workload

---

# Project Goals

The project aims to automate repetitive recruitment tasks while improving collaboration and candidate experience.

---

# Functional Requirements

The solution should:

- Accept online job applications
- Store candidate information automatically
- Send confirmation emails
- Create Trello recruitment cards
- Track applicants through hiring stages
- Support interview scheduling
- Support offer management
- Support onboarding activities

---

# Non-Functional Requirements

- Easy to maintain
- No-code implementation
- Scalable
- Cloud-based
- User-friendly
- Reliable

---

# Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- Applications are processed automatically
- Recruiters no longer perform duplicate data entry
- Candidates receive immediate confirmation emails
- Trello is automatically updated
- Recruitment status is visible at every stage
