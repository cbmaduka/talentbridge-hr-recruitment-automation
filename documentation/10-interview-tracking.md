# Interview Tracking Automation

## Objective

The Interview Tracking Automation records candidate progression after interviews have been completed.

It provides recruiters with a standardized process for documenting interview outcomes and maintaining accurate recruitment records.

---

# Trigger

**Application**

Trello

**Event**

Card Moved to List

**List**

Interviewed

---

# Automated Actions

The automation:

- Detects completed interviews
- Updates recruitment records
- Synchronizes interview status
- Maintains consistent candidate tracking

---

# Business Benefits

- Standardized interview documentation
- Improved recruitment visibility
- Better reporting
- Reduced administrative work

---

# Outcome

Candidate interview progress is automatically tracked, reducing manual updates while ensuring accurate recruitment records.
