# Phone Screening Automation

## Objective

The Phone Screening Automation streamlines the transition from application review to the initial candidate interview.

When a recruiter moves a candidate into the **Phone Screening** stage, Zapier automatically performs predefined actions to support the next step in the recruitment process.

---

# Trigger

**Application**

Trello

**Event**

Card Moved to List

**List**

Phone Screening

---

# Automated Actions

The automation performs the following actions:

- Detects when a candidate enters the Phone Screening stage
- Creates a Google Calendar interview event
- Updates the recruitment workflow
- Supports interview scheduling activities

---

# Business Benefits

- Reduces manual scheduling
- Standardizes interview preparation
- Improves recruiter productivity
- Ensures consistent recruitment processes

---

# Outcome

Recruiters only need to move the candidate card to the **Phone Screening** list. The automation handles the next stage of the workflow automatically.
