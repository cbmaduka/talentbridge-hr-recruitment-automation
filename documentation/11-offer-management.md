# Offer Management Automation

## Objective

The Offer Management Automation standardizes the transition from a successful interview to the job offer stage.

When a recruiter moves a candidate to the **Offer Sent** list, the automation updates the recruitment workflow, ensuring the candidate's status is accurately reflected throughout the hiring process.

---

# Trigger

**Application**

Trello

**Event**

Card Moved to List

**List**

Offer Sent

---

# Automated Actions

The automation performs the following actions:

- Detects when a candidate reaches the Offer Sent stage
- Updates the recruitment workflow
- Records the candidate's progression
- Supports consistent offer management

---

# Business Benefits

- Standardized hiring workflow
- Improved recruitment visibility
- Reduced manual status updates
- Better collaboration among recruiters

---

# Outcome

Recruiters only need to move a candidate card to the **Offer Sent** list. The automation updates the workflow, reducing manual administration while maintaining accurate recruitment records.
